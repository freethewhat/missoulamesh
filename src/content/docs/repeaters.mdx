A **repeater** is a MeshCore radio that passes messages along so they travel farther. Your device needs to hear at least one repeater to reach other people.

The Missoula mesh is a small network. The map shows where each repeater is and where people have reached one. The list gives each repeater's neighborhood and status.

## Coverage map

<CoverageMap>Most of the south side, around the Lewis and Clark and Franklin to the Fort neighborhoods, reached a repeater. So did parts of Orchard Homes. Other areas have not been tested yet.</CoverageMap>

**What the labels mean**

- **Testing:** it is up, but we are still checking it.
- **On the air:** it is working.
- **Down:** it is off right now.
- **Status unknown:** we are not sure if it is working.

**No repeater near you?** You may still reach one from high ground. You can also [tell us where you are](/contact/), so we know where the next repeater should go.
