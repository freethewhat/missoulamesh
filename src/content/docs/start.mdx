This takes about 15 minutes. You will set up a **companion**: the MeshCore device you pair with your phone.

<ol class="mm-steps">
  <li>
    **What you need.** A MeshCore-compatible radio (see [what to buy](/hardware/)), a phone with the MeshCore app, and a computer with Chrome or Edge.
    Get the free MeshCore app for [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.liamcottle.meshcore.android) or [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/meshcore/id6742354151). You can also use the [web app](https://app.meshcore.nz/) on a computer.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Flash the companion firmware.** To **flash** means to put new software on the radio. Plug the radio into your computer. Open [flasher.meshcore.io](https://flasher.meshcore.io/) in Chrome or Edge. Pick your device, then pick the "Companion" firmware.
    If your radio came with MeshCore on it, you can skip this step.
    <Aside title="Have a Meshtastic radio?">
      Most Meshtastic radios can run MeshCore too. Flashing MeshCore replaces Meshtastic. See [how to switch a Meshtastic radio](/meshtastic/#already-have-a-meshtastic-radio).
    </Aside>
  </li>
  <li id="pair">
    **Pair the app and set the radio.** Open the MeshCore app and connect to your radio over Bluetooth. In the radio settings, choose the preset **{settings.radio.presetName}**. Set the path hash size to **{pathHashLabel(settings.pathHashMode)}**. Leave the default region scope blank.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Join #missoula and say hello.** Add the channel <code>#missoula</code> in the app. Type it with the `#`. Send a short message, then wait a few minutes for a reply. If you see one, you are on the mesh.
    Quiet? Try the [room server](/room/). It keeps old messages.
  </li>
</ol>

No reply, or stuck on a step? See [when it does not work](/help/). Still stuck? Use the [contact form](/contact/) and tell us what you see. We will help.

All your device settings are on one page: [device settings](/settings/).
