This takes about 15 minutes. You will set up a **companion**: the MeshCore device you pair with your phone.

<ol class="mm-steps">
  <li>
    **What you need.** A MeshCore-compatible radio, a phone with the MeshCore app, and a computer with Chrome or Edge.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Check the repeater list.** A **repeater** is a radio, often up high, that passes messages along. Look at the [repeater list](/repeaters/) to see if one is near you.
    If none is near you: try from high ground, or use the [contact form](/contact/) to tell us where you are. You could also host a repeater yourself.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Flash the companion firmware.** To **flash** means to put new software on the radio. Plug the radio into your computer. Open [flasher.meshcore.io](https://flasher.meshcore.io/) in Chrome or Edge. Pick your device, then pick the "Companion" firmware.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Pair the app and set the radio.** Open the MeshCore app and connect to your radio over Bluetooth. In the radio settings, choose the preset **{settings.radio.presetName}**. Set the path hash size to **{pathHashLabel(settings.pathHashMode)}**. Leave the default region scope blank.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Join the Missoula channel.** Add the channel <code>#missoula</code> in the app. Type it with the `#`.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Say hello.** Send a short message on <code>#missoula</code>. Wait a few minutes and look for a reply. If you see one, you are on the mesh.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Stuck?** Use the [contact form](/contact/) and tell us what you see. We will help.
  </li>
</ol>

All your device settings are on one page: [device settings](/settings/).
