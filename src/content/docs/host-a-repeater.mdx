You do not need a repeater to use the mesh. But if you want to help it grow, this page has some tips.

## What a repeater does

A **repeater** is a MeshCore radio that passes messages along. It does not pair with a phone. It just listens and sends messages on.

Height matters more than anything else. A repeater on a roof, a pole, or a hill can reach much farther than one in a window. Each new repeater helps everyone around it.

## Setting it up

1. **Connect to it.** Plug the repeater into a computer. Open [config.meshcore.io](https://config.meshcore.io/) in Chrome or Edge.
2. **Match the valley settings.** Your repeater must use the same radio preset as everyone else: **{settings.radio.presetName}**. Set the path hash size to **{pathHashLabel(settings.pathHashMode)}**. Both are on the [device settings](/settings/) page.
3. **Change the admin password.** The default is <code>password</code>. Anyone nearby can log in to your repeater until you change it.
4. **Give it a name.** A tip: use the area name, not your street address.
5. **Location is up to you.** A repeater can share its location on maps. If you share it, use a rough spot, not your exact house.
6. **Leave the advert settings at their defaults.** An **advert** is a short message where the repeater says "I am here." The defaults keep the air quiet.

After that, you can manage the repeater from the MeshCore app over the radio. Log in to it as admin. Some versions of the app charge a small fee for this.

## Stay legal

Do not add an amplifier. Radios that need no license in the US have power limits, and an amplifier can go over them.
