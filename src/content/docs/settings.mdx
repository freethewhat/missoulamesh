These are the settings for your own device: the one you pair with your phone. Every device in the valley needs the same settings, or they cannot hear each other.

## In the MeshCore app

1. **Radio preset:** choose **{settings.radio.presetName}**. This one choice sets the radio for you.
2. **Path hash size:** choose **{pathHashLabel(settings.pathHashMode)}**. Find it under network settings. (The **path hash** is a short ID each repeater adds to a message as it passes it on.)
3. **Default region scope:** leave it **blank**. (A **region** keeps messages local. Setting one too early can cut you off from repeaters.)

## Radio settings

The preset fills these in for you. If your app has no preset list, enter them by hand.

<RadioSettings />

## Channels

A **channel** is a group chat. Everyone on the same channel can read it. Add `#missoula` first.

<ChannelTable />

The key is only for devices that cannot type `#`. On most devices, type the name and the app works out the key.
