---
title: When it does not work
description: Fixes for common problems when you set up a MeshCore device.
---

Most problems have a simple fix. Find what you see below.

## The app cannot find the radio

- Make sure you flashed the **Companion** firmware with Bluetooth. Other firmware cannot pair with a phone.
- If the phone asks for a code, try `123456`. That is the default.
- Remove the radio from your phone's Bluetooth settings. Then connect again from inside the MeshCore app.

## The flasher cannot see the radio

- Use Chrome or Edge. Other browsers cannot talk to the radio.
- Try a different USB cable. Some cables only charge and do not carry data.
- On Linux, your user may need permission to use the USB port. See the [MeshCore FAQ](https://docs.meshcore.io/faq/).

## No reply on #missoula

- Check your settings against the [device settings](/settings/) page. One wrong setting and you cannot hear anyone.
- Look at the [repeater list](/repeaters/). If no repeater is near you, try from higher ground.
- Tap **Advert** in the app. This tells the mesh you are here.
- People may not be online right now. Try again later in the day.

## No repeaters in my list

- Tap **Advert** in the app and wait a few minutes.
- Check that your phone's clock is correct.

## Start fresh

If nothing works, flash the radio again from the start.

Only do a full erase when you want a fresh start. A full erase deletes your MeshCore identity, and your contacts will see you as a new person. When you only want an update, do not erase.

## Still stuck?

Use the [contact form](/contact/) and tell us what you see. We will help. The [MeshCore FAQ](https://docs.meshcore.io/faq/) has more answers too.
