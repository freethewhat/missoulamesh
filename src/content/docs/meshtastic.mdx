---
title: Looking for Meshtastic?
description: Meshtastic users in western Montana should join Ravalli Mesh.
---

**Meshtastic** is a different mesh radio system. MeshCore and Meshtastic radios cannot talk to each other, even on the same hardware.

If you want Meshtastic, join our neighbors at **Ravalli Mesh**. They run a good Meshtastic network in the Bitterroot Valley, and it reaches toward Missoula.

- [Ravalli Mesh home page](https://ravallimesh.org/)
- [Join the community](https://ravallimesh.org/join-the-community/)
- [Their Missoula and Frenchtown coverage](https://ravallimesh.org/new-coverage-missoula-frenchtown/)
- [Live node map](https://mesh.ravallimesh.org/)
- [Chat (Zulip)](https://go.ravallimesh.org/s/chat)
- [Best practices](https://ravallimesh.org/best-practices/)

They also hold monthly meetups in Hamilton. Check [their site](https://ravallimesh.org/) for dates.
