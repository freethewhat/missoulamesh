---
title: Looking for Meshtastic?
description: Meshtastic users in western Montana should join Ravalli Mesh.
---

**Meshtastic** is a different mesh radio system. MeshCore and Meshtastic radios cannot talk to each other, even on the same hardware.

If you want Meshtastic, join our neighbors at **Ravalli Mesh**. They run a good Meshtastic network in the Bitterroot Valley, and it reaches toward Missoula.

They also hold monthly meetups in Hamilton. Check the [Ravalli Mesh site](https://ravallimesh.org/) for links, chat, and meetup dates.
