**Meshtastic** is a different mesh radio system. MeshCore and Meshtastic radios cannot talk to each other, even on the same hardware.

If you want Meshtastic, join our neighbors at **Ravalli Mesh**. They run a good Meshtastic network in the Bitterroot Valley, and it reaches toward Missoula.

They also hold monthly meetups in Hamilton. Check the [Ravalli Mesh site](https://ravallimesh.org/) for links, chat, and meetup dates.

## Already have a Meshtastic radio?

You can probably switch it to MeshCore. Most radios that run Meshtastic can run MeshCore too. Look for your radio in the device list on [flasher.meshcore.io](https://flasher.meshcore.io/).

To switch:

1. Write down anything you want to keep from Meshtastic. Flashing MeshCore removes Meshtastic and its settings.
2. Plug the radio into your computer. Open [flasher.meshcore.io](https://flasher.meshcore.io/) in Chrome or Edge.
3. Pick your device, then pick the "Companion" firmware. If the flasher offers to erase the radio first, we suggest you do that. It clears out the old Meshtastic files.
4. Then [pair the app and set the radio](/start/#pair).

Changed your mind? You can put Meshtastic back at any time with the [Meshtastic flasher](https://flasher.meshtastic.org/).
