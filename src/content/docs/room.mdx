The mesh is quiet right now. If your radio is off when someone posts on <code>#missoula</code>, you miss it. So we are trying a **room server**.

This is an experiment. It may change or go away.

## What a room server does

A **room server** is a MeshCore radio that keeps messages. When you log in, it sends you the posts you missed. You can see what people said last week. You can also leave a note for the next person who stops by.

<RoomServer />

## How to join

<ol class="mm-steps">
  <li>
    **Add the room as a contact.** In the MeshCore app, scan the QR code above. On a phone with the app, you can also tap the link.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Log in.** Open the room from your contacts. Leave the password blank.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Catch up, then say hello.** Read back through the old posts. Then post a short hello so others know you are here.
  </li>
</ol>

## Why use the QR code

The room says "I am here" across the whole mesh only once a day. You may wait up to a day to see it on your own. The QR code or link adds it right away.

## Can you reach it?

The room is near the Lewis and Clark repeater. If your radio reaches that repeater, you should reach the room too. See the [repeater list](/repeaters/).

## A few tips

- Anyone can log in, read, and post. Do not post anything private.
- Be kind. This is a place for new people to land.
- Tell us what you think on the [contact form](/contact/). It helps us decide if the room stays.
