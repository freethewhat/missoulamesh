To use MeshCore with your phone, you need a **companion**. A companion is a small radio that you pair with your phone over Bluetooth. Some come with MeshCore already on them. Others you flash yourself. To **flash** means to put the software on the radio.

## Tips

- Buy the US version. It uses **915 MHz** (sometimes listed as 902-928 MHz).
- Do not turn a radio on without its antenna attached. This can damage the radio.
- Prices change. The date shows when we last checked.
