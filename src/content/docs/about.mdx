Missoula Mesh is a small group of neighbors who want a MeshCore network in the valley. It is a volunteer project. There is no company and nothing to buy.

The goal is simple: help people get on the mesh, and help the mesh grow.

Use the [contact form](/contact/), or email **hello@missoulamesh.org**.
